About The Coach

Dedicated to Excellence in Taekwondo

Haykaz Samvelyan is an internationally certified Taekwondo coach from Armenia, renowned for his European Championship gold medal and unwavering commitment to developing world-class athletes.

With years of competitive experience and a deep understanding of modern Taekwondo training methodologies, Coach Haykaz has trained hundreds of students across all age groups and skill levels, guiding them to achieve their highest potential in both sport and character.
