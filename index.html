About The Coach

Meet Haykaz Samvelyan

🥋
Coach

Haykaz Samvelyan is a distinguished Armenian Taekwondo Coach, European champion, and the founder of the "Tigran Mets" Taekwondo Club in Yerevan.

With over 15 years of dedicated training and competition experience, Haykaz has developed powerful martial artists and continues to inspire the next generation of Armenian champions. His club, named after the legendary Armenian king Tigran the Great, embodies strength, discipline, and the warrior spirit.

Based at Mesrop Mashtots Ave. 5/2 in the heart of Yerevan, the Tigran Mets club offers comprehensive Taekwondo training for students of all ages and skill levels.

Training Philosophy

  • Discipline and mental toughness
  • Competition preparation for all levels
  • Character building through martial arts
  • All ages welcome — children to adults
